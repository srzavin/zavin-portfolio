Introduction

In the evolving landscape of tech and health care, safeguarding patient data is of the highest priority. HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) of 1996 plays a crucial role in ensuring the confidentiality of individuals' health information. Enforcement of HIPAA has become more stringent amid evolving challenges, technologies, and digital threats.

HIPAA Obligations for App Developers:

  • Covered Entities vs. Business Associates: App developers may need to comply with HIPAA if they function as business associates working with covered entities (e.g., healthcare providers or insurers).
  • Access to PHI: If an app handles protected health information (PHI) on behalf of covered entities, the developer is obligated to comply with HIPAA's privacy and security rules.
  • Security Measures: Implementing robust security measures, encryption, and ensuring data integrity become crucial for developers handling PHI.
